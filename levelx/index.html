A flat monthly fee — the same figure every month, regardless of how the days fall. Charged at $60.00 per labour hour, with three hours allocated to each daily visit. The three-hour allocation refers to total daily labour hours; where appropriate, Elevex may allocate more than one team member to complete the service efficiently within the same total allocation.

Recommended before you start: a one-time deep clean — soft wash of natural stone surfaces, a full tile and grout deep clean, and a general deep clean throughout the club — so daily servicing starts from a genuinely reset baseline rather than working through an accumulated backlog. $1,100.00 + GST, one-time fee.

The detail

Service notes

  • Scope based on the Cleaning Scope of Works document supplied by Level X Club, confirmed with Louise Ingaldo and Emil Juresic.
  • All rates shown are exclusive of GST; totals above include GST at invoicing in line with standard practice.
  • Bin liners, soap, toilet paper and paper towel are supplied by Level X Club; Elevex restocks these at each scheduled service, or can supply and invoice them separately on request.
  • Days and timing are indicative and can be adjusted in collaboration with the Level X team to suit site operations; any change to tasks, frequencies or areas is agreed in writing between both parties.
  • Cleaning staff will not move or relocate furniture, documents or equipment unless directed by Level X.
  • Dishes are not cleaned due to hygiene and liability considerations — where a dishwasher is fitted, staff will load it.
  • Cleaning products and professional equipment are supplied by Elevex Property Solutions unless otherwise agreed.
A note on windows: the monthly inclusion covers internal glass only. External window cleaning is not part of the daily service or the flat monthly fee, and is quoted separately — see Quoted separately.
Outside standard scope: the rates above cover routine, day-to-day servicing. Major or one-off soiling — from events, incidents, renovation dust or heavy usage spikes — falls outside the standard scope and will be assessed and quoted separately before any extra work proceeds.
